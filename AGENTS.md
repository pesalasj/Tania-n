# Agent Guidelines & Custom Instructions

When providing information from the internet or any other source, all information must be verified and valid. All the phone numbers given and websites must be working and updated information.

1. **Information Verification & Accuracy**: Always verify and ensure any facts, contact information, phone numbers, or website URLs provided in the workspace, app code, or by the voice assistant are completely active, working, and thoroughly up-to-date.
2. **No Fictional Placeholders**: Avoid outputting or utilizing fake or mock placeholder numbers (such as "123-456-7890" or "555-xxxx") or unverified broken domains/URLs (such as "example.com" or mock email addresses) in application UI messages or assistant directives.
